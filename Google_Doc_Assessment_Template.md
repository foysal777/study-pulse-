## Important Rules to Follow:
1. **[TEMPLATE]**: The document must begin with `[TEMPLATE]` followed by the name of the level on the very next line.
2. **[SECTION: skill_name]**: You must define sections using this format. The supported skills are: `grammar`, `vocabulary`, `reading`, and `listening`.
3. **Correct Answer**: Mark the correct option by placing an asterisk (`*`) at the end of the line (e.g., `B) went *`).
4. **Options**: Provide 3 or 4 options starting with `A)`, `B)`, `C)`, `D)` (or `A.`, `B.`, `C.`, `D.`).
5. **Spacing**: Leave a blank line between one question and the next question.
6. **Passages**: If the section (like reading or listening) has a passage or general instruction text before the questions, place it right under `[PASSAGE]`.
